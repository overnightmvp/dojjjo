**Melbourne West Market Characteristics:**
- Working-class demographics with strong multicultural communities

## Implementation Recommendations

### Door-to-Door Campaign Strategy
**Week 1-2: Footscray Focus**
- Target Vietnamese restaurants along Hopkins St
- Approach during quiet hours (2-4 PM)
- Lead with commission savings message

**Week 3-4: Williamstown/Altona**
- Focus on family-owned establishments  
- Emphasize heritage and community connection
- Weekend morning visits work best

**Week 5-6: Werribee/Sunshine**
- Mix of all approaches based on restaurant type
- Shopping center restaurants during weekday mornings
- Independent locations during afternoon lulls

### Conversion Optimization
1. **Local Social Proof** - Get 2-3 pilot customers before major push
2. **Suburb-Specific Landing Pages** - Create versions for each area
3. **Mobile-First Design** - Most owners will view on phones
4. **Bilingual Options** - At minimum, offer Vietnamese translations
5. **Local SEO Focus** - Target "restaurant website [suburb]" keywords

### Follow-Up Sequences
**Day 1:** Thank you + website preview
**Day 3:** How-to video for dashboard  
**Week 1:** Success metrics from other local restaurants
**Week 2:** Additional services upsell
**Month 1:** Customer success check-in and testimonial request
